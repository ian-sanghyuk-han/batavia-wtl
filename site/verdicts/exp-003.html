Credit leads equities — does a HY-spread shock predict weak weeks?

Hypothesis: a sharp 5-day widening of the credit spread (≥1.5σ, trailing) precedes weaker S&P 500 weeks.

Verdict: forward 5-day returns after 140 credit-shock signals averaged 0.064% vs 0.179% on ordinary days — the hypothesized direction, but p=0.2755: not significant under the preregistered test. Scope note: this verdict binds THIS proxy (Baa−10Y) at THIS horizon; a true HY-OAS retest awaits data we are now archiving daily.
